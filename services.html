Services

Four areas of practice for engineered products and IT hardware: technical compliance evaluation, engineering safety & engineering standards, design evaluation, and market readiness.

Technical Compliance Evaluation

Does the delivered or offered product actually comply with the standard and the spec it is sold against? Verified with evidence, before acceptance.

  • Certificate & test-report verification — ratings, marks, CB reports checked against the governing standard
  • Compliance gap analysis — product vs IEC, EN, ISO, UL/CSA, NEMA, with fixes listed
  • Tender & spec compliance matrices — clause-by-clause, for bids and purchase orders
  • FAT, burn-in & type-test management — witnessed factory acceptance (incl. HV/MV machines), burn-in and Ex test programs, third-party lab coordination

Engineering Safety & Engineering Standards

The standards landscape for each product family — and the safety failure modes those standards exist to prevent.

  • Standards mapping — product to governing engineering & safety standards, per market
  • Safety reviews — arc-flash, hazardous areas (ATEX/IECEx), functional safety (IEC 61511 / SIL)
  • Standards-revision watch — changes in IEC/EN/UL editions that affect your products
  • Incident-driven advisories — what documented failures mean for your specification

Design Evaluation

Independent review of designs and offered products against the standard — and against the alternatives — before money is committed.

  • Design review to standard — e.g., IEC 61439 design verification, IEC 60034 ratings and derating
  • Drawings, calculations & technical-file review — completeness and correctness
  • Comparative benchmarking — up to four designs or products, technical + commercial matrix
  • Alternates & value engineering — cost out, compliance preserved, equivalents qualified

Market Readiness

Everything between a compliant product and a product that can legally be sold, shipped, and accepted in the target market.

  • Conformity routes — CE/UKCA, UL/CSA, SASO/SABER, G-Mark/GCC, PSQCA
  • Marine & Ex approvals — class type approval (DNV, ABS, Lloyd’s Register), MED wheelmark, ATEX/IECEx routes
  • Technical files, declarations & marking — DoC, SDS, labeling done right
  • Certification & test-lab coordination — scoping and managing UL, TÜV, SGS, Intertek programs
  • Registration & import documentation — platform filings and border paperwork support
  • Defense & NATO qualification — NCAGE registration, NSN codification, MIL-STD/AQAP conformity, QPL entry

Supporting services on request: NSN/NATO sourcing across an 18M-item catalogue, price & award intelligence, and Shipley/APMP bid support. See Pricing.
