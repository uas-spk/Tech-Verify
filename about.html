The principal

Umer Siddiqui is a mechatronics engineer (B.Eng., NUST) who has spent 18+ years on the technical-commercial boundary of engineered products: Siemens in Pakistan and Saudi Arabia — including oil & gas automation bids for a Gulf national oil company and kingdom-wide channel management — a Gulf lubricants major, co-founding KPL, a system-integration business, and founding KEVEREST in the USA & Canada as a direct B2G/B2B supplier and bids consultancy.

That path covers every seat at the table: the OEM selling against a spec, the integrator building to one, the proposal manager defending one, and the government supplier audited against one. TECHVERIFY turns that vantage into a service — verifying products against the standards that govern them before money and reputation are committed.

How an engagement runs

  • MapProduct to governing standards and the target market's conformity route.
  • VerifyRatings, test reports, certificates and markings checked against the standard.
  • EvaluateBenchmark against alternates on technical merit and commercial terms.
  • DecideA written recommendation with the evidence — buy, fix, or walk away.

Selected engagements

  • SAR 450M — automation order for 638 well sites for a Gulf national oil company; largest in the department's history at award.
  • 17 bulk plants — terminal-management upgrade programs across a national fuels network, with parts agreements protecting the installed base.
  • 55% IACS — busbar award conditional on verified aluminium conductivity; extrusion factory inspected before acceptance.
  • CAD 5M — new federal/SLED orders as a direct government supplier, screened through in-house Bid/No-Bid and win-probability models.
  • PKR 40M — first full high-efficiency (IE-class) motor revamp in a national market, won on the energy-standards case.
  • 5 machines, witnessed in Germany — factory acceptance tests for three high-voltage and two medium-voltage motors at the manufacturer’s works: heat runs, efficiency and vibration to IEC 60034 / ISO 20816, with third-party test coordination.
  • Burn-in & Ex programs — burn-in and explosion-proof test requirements managed for oil & gas and offshore projects of a Gulf national oil company, from test plan through witnessed acceptance.
  • Own product, type-tested — developed an aluminium busbar after employment and took it through type testing against aluminium-busbar standards: conductor conductivity to % IACS, temperature-rise and short-circuit verification per IEC 61439.

Methods: Shipley / APMP-aligned capture and proposals. Client identities withheld by design; references available in a scoped discussion.
