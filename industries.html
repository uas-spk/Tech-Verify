Industries

Twenty buyer worlds, each with its own rulebook — and the route is set by the product, so an industry not listed here is handled the same way. The engagement examples below are drawn from the principal's delivered work, with client identities masked.

Government & Defense

Federal, state/provincial and defense buyers demand codified, traceable, compliant supply — from NSN-coded parts to supplier qualification and country-of-origin rules.

NSN / NCAGEFED / SLEDDoD · MOD · DND · NATOSupplier qualification

Engagement example: built a direct federal/SLED supply business in North America to CAD 5M in new orders across its first aggressive fiscal years, using in-house Bid/No-Bid and win-probability models — every pursuit screened on numbers before a dollar of proposal effort was spent.

Oil, Gas & Energy

Hazardous areas, functional safety, and installed-base continuity — where a certificate is a barrier against ignition and an obsolete part is a production risk.

ATEX / IECExIEC 60079IEC 61511 / SILBrownfield upgrades

Plants buy motors, drives, switchgear and busbar on specification — and pay for the gap whenever a delivered product misses the spec it was sold against.
