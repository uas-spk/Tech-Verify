Industries

Four buyer worlds, each with its own rulebook. The engagement examples below are drawn from the principal's delivered work, with client identities masked.

Government & Defense

Federal, state/provincial and defense buyers demand codified, traceable, compliant supply — from NSN-coded parts to supplier qualification and country-of-origin rules.

NSN / NCAGEFED / SLEDDoD · MOD · DND · NATOSupplier qualification

Engagement example: built a direct federal/SLED supply business in North America to CAD 5M in new orders across its first aggressive fiscal years, using in-house Bid/No-Bid and win-probability models — every pursuit screened on numbers before a dollar of proposal effort was spent.

Oil, Gas & Energy

Hazardous areas, functional safety, and installed-base continuity — where a certificate is a barrier against ignition and an obsolete part is a production risk.

ATEX / IECExIEC 60079IEC 61511 / SILBrownfield upgrades

Engagement example: for a Gulf national oil company, managed automation bids from FEED through award — including a 638-well-site automation order worth SAR 450M, the department's largest to date — and ran upgrade programs across 17 terminal bulk plants, with parts-management agreements keeping the installed base supported.

Industrial & Manufacturing

Plants buy motors, drives, switchgear and busbar on specification — and pay for the gap whenever a delivered product misses the spec it was sold against.

IEC 60034 / 61800IEC 61439IE efficiencyFactory inspection

Engagement example: an EPC's busbar award was conditional on the aluminium conductor achieving a minimum 55% IACS conductivity — verified through extrusion-factory inspection before acceptance. Separately, introduced high-efficiency (IE-class) motors to a national market, converting the energy-standards case into its first full plant revamp worth PKR 40M.

Construction & MEP

Envelope, electrical, mechanical and plumbing packages each carry their own conformity route — and site acceptance is where undocumented products get rejected.

CE / UKCASASO / SABERG-Mark · GCCEN 14351 · EN 13501

Engagement example: delivered lighting and power distribution boards for a diplomatic mission facility and dozens of LV distribution boards for a major industrial plant through an EPC — every assembly supplied against verified specifications and accepted at site without rework.
