CONTR-ACTING

Contracting is a performance. I have played every part.

Most consultants advise from outside the arena. I am in it — bidding in real time for my own company, in the same portals, to the same buyers, against the same evaluators as my clients. And when an evaluation was wrong, I challenged it myself: bid protests filed before Canada’s trade tribunal, the submissions drafted and argued personally, without counsel. Two determinations are matters of public record — CITT file PR-2024-043 and PR-2026-001. In the second, the Tribunal held the complaint valid and awarded both bid-preparation costs and complaint costs against the institution.

If I can act for myself against a tribunal and win, and bid for myself and win — then you have no reason to sit out the tender. You have every reason to bring in someone who has already stood exactly where you are standing.
