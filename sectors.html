Sectors & Standards

Twelve engineered-product domains, each mapped to the engineering and safety standards that govern it — and the failure modes those standards exist to prevent. Hover any tag for the issuing body, country of origin and marking — click it to open the official source.

Rotating Machines & Drives

LV/MV motors, variable-frequency drives, soft starters.

IEC 60034IEC 60034-30 (IE)NEMA MG-1IEC 61800IEC 61800-3 EMCUL 61800-5-1ATEX / IECEx (Ex motors)Marine class (DNV · ABS · LR)

Safety context: locked-rotor and overload conditions overheat windings and start fires; unfiltered drives disturb networks and trip protection. Thermal protection, efficiency class, and EMC conformity are verifiable — before the order, not after the failure.

Power Distribution & Switchgear

LV & MV switchgear, distribution boards, busbar trunking, panelboards.

IEC 61439IEC 61439-6IEC 62271ANSI/IEEE C37IEC 60364UL 891 / 1558IEC 60092 (ships)UL 857 (busway)ASTM B236 / B317BS 2898% IACS (ASTM B193)

Safety context: arc-flash events injure electrical workers every year, and counterfeit or unverified breakers have been the subject of repeated industry recalls. Design verification (temperature rise, short-circuit withstand) under IEC 61439 is exactly what separates an assembly from a box of parts.

Cables & Cable Management

LV/MV power cable, control & instrumentation cable, cable tray and ladder.

IEC 60228IEC 60502IEC 60332EU CPR (EN 50575)NEMA VE-1IEC 61537UL listedIEC 60092-350 (marine)

Safety context: substandard and counterfeit cable — undersized conductors, poor insulation — has repeatedly failed flame-propagation tests in market surveillance. The EU classifies cables for fire behaviour under CPR precisely because cabling is how fire and smoke travel through a building.

Instrumentation & Control

Field instruments, transmitters, analyzers, control systems, hazardous-area equipment.

IEC 61010IEC 60079ATEX / IECExIEC 61511SILClass/Div (NEC · CEC)UL / FM haz-locDNV type approval

Safety context: the Buncefield terminal explosion (UK, 2005) followed a tank overfill after level instrumentation and its independent safety switch failed to act. Hazardous-area certification and safety-instrumented-function integrity are not paperwork — they are the last barrier.

HVAC & Mechanical Systems

Chillers, AHUs, rooftop units, fans, split systems, filtration.

AHRIASHRAEEN 14511Ecodesign / ErPF-gasISO 16890UL / CSAMarine class rules

Safety context: the industry shift to lower-GWP refrigerants brings mildly flammable A2L classes into occupied buildings, and poorly maintained cooling towers remain a documented Legionella pathway. Refrigerant class, charge limits, and hygiene standards decide what may be installed where.

Building Envelope

Windows, doors, curtain wall, awnings, cladding interfaces.

EN 14351EN 13501 (fire)CE / UKCAAAMA / NAFSEN 1627 (security)

Safety context: the Grenfell Tower fire (London, 2017) showed how non-compliant façade and envelope products turn a contained incident into a catastrophe. Fire classification and system-level test evidence for envelope products is now scrutinized in every serious market.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Valves, fittings, pipe systems, water heaters, sanitaryware.

NSF/ANSI 61WRASUPC / IPCEN 806Low-lead

Safety context: the Flint water crisis (USA, 2014–) made drinking-water contact materials a public-health issue, driving low-lead requirements into law. Certification of wetted materials and backflow protection is what keeps a water system potable.

Polymers, Plastics & Chemical Products

Compounds and masterbatch, mouldings and extrusions, films and packaging, pipes and fittings, coatings, adhesives, resins, lubricants and industrial chemicals.

REACH / CLP-GHSISO 527 / ISO 178UL 94 / IEC 60695ASTM D638 / D1238EU 10/2011 · FDA 21 CFRISO 4427 / EN 12201 (PE pipe)SASO / G-Mark

Safety context: with a material the product is the composition, so conformity turns on what can be measured and what can migrate — flammability rating, tensile and melt-flow behaviour, food-contact migration limits, restricted substances, and a safety data sheet that classifies the hazard correctly. Grenfell put polymer flammability and its certification under a public inquiry; food-contact recalls turn on migration testing nobody ran; and a shipment whose SDS or CLP labelling does not match its classification is stopped at the border regardless of product quality.

Electronics, Appliances & Chemicals

IT hardware, AIDC, consumer & commercial appliances, batteries, chemical products.

CE (LVD/EMC)IEC 60335IEC 62368-1UL 2272RoHS / WEEEREACH / CLP-GHS

Safety context: lithium-battery fires — from the hoverboard wave that produced UL 2272 to today's e-mobility incidents — and repeated appliance fire recalls show what uncertified electronics cost. Chemical products without proper CLP/GHS classification and SDS are a liability at every border.

Defense & NATO-Codified Products

NSN-coded items across the ~18M-item NATO catalogue — electronics, spares, field and platform equipment.

NSN / NCAGEMIL-STD / MIL-PRFDEF STANSTANAG / AQAPQPL / QMLDFARS counterfeit rules

Safety context: official investigations have documented counterfeit electronic parts deep inside defense supply chains — which is why approved-source verification, NSN-to-CAGE traceability and QPL discipline are mandatory controls, and why export regimes (ITAR/EAR) punish missteps in both directions. Every part must trace to an approved source, or it does not fly.

Marine, Offshore & Shipping

Shipboard and offshore electrical & mechanical equipment, navigation and safety gear.

DNV · ABS · Lloyd's RegisterBV · RINA · ClassNKIMO / SOLASMED wheelmarkIEC 60092IEC 60533 (EMC)

Safety context: SOLAS — first written after the Titanic — still governs life at sea, and classification societies enforce it: equipment without class type approval or the MED wheelmark does not sail on a classed vessel. Offshore units layer their own rules on top, and surveyors reject undocumented kit at the quay.

Hazardous-Area & Explosion-Proof (Ex)

Ex-rated motors, luminaires, junction boxes, instruments, HVAC and enclosures for gas and dust atmospheres.

ATEX 2014/34/EUIECExIEC/EN 60079NEC 500/505 · CECUL / FM approvalsEx d · e · i · t

Safety context: from refinery vapour clouds to grain-dust explosions, ignition inside a classified area is catastrophic. Equipment must carry the exact Ex marking for the zone, gas group and temperature class — and mismatched or partial certificates are one of the most routine audit failures in industry.

Certification groups, segregated

Different bodies do different jobs. Knowing which group owns your requirement is half the route: standards writers set the rules, marks and regimes open markets, class societies clear ships, Ex schemes clear zones, labs test, and defense qualifies its own way.
